# Executive Insights for AIRMAN Training Intelligence
This summary highlights the top operational, training and finance signals for Skynet and TOGA.

## Top Operational Insights
- Dispatch reliability is 76.0%, indicating most sorties are executed or delayed rather than cancelled.
- Several aircraft have utilization below 50%, suggesting a reserve capacity that can be optimized with better maintenance scheduling.

## Training Insights
- 18 cadets are under 60% flight completion and should be prioritized for additional simulator and cross-country blocks.
- Lesson types such as instrument flying and cross-country contribute the largest average delay.

## TOGA Personalization Opportunities
- Weak subjects should drive personalized TOGA content bundles in Aerodynamics, Air Law and Navigation.
- Inactivity risk cadets should receive automated reminders and focused practice tests.

## Finance Risks
- Total outstanding training fees are material and correlate with lower training progress for several cadets.
- High outstanding payment ratios alongside low completion progress are strong continuity risk signals.

## Product Recommendations
- Use Skynet to monitor underutilized aircraft and redirect maintenance slots toward heavier bases.
- Use TOGA readiness scores to trigger coach interventions and personalized test packs.

## Data Quality Concerns
- Several records require cleansing for delays, cancellations and study progress values.

## Additional data to collect
- Weather impacts per sortie, flight instructor feedback scores, and simulation hours would improve risk models.
- Cadet wellbeing and attendance logs would support a richer training continuity view.

## Final recommendation
- Prioritize the top 10% highest-risk cadets for immediate intervention, while using underutilized aircraft capacity to accelerate on-track trainees.
